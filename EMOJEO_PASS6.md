# Emojeo Pass 6 — Vocabulary Lab

Pass 6 adds the corpus-wide vocabulary stage above Pass 5's lossless open-discovery corpus.

- Repeated raw phrases are counted across emoji without collapsing distinct concepts.
- Synonym/concept families can be proposed with a suggested canonical label.
- Proposals are explicitly `proposed`, `approved`, or `rejected` and preserve source/rationale/confidence.
- Machine suggestions remain advisory. Nothing becomes an Emojeo trait merely because an algorithm proposed it.
- Only an explicitly approved proposal can materialize a curated `emojeo-trait` and its subject assignments.
- Raw discovery observations are never rewritten or deleted by vocabulary decisions.
- Unicode Group/Subgroup remain authoritative traits from Pass 5 repair and coexist with curated/discovered traits in StringBoard.

This pass provides the normalization/curation machinery; it does not invent the actual Emojeo vocabulary yet.
