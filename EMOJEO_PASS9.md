# Emojeo Pass 9 — Mosaic Candidate Review

Pass 9 adds the human decision boundary between Pass 8 discovery and canonical Mosaics.

- Advisory `mosaic-candidate` records enter a review board as **proposed**.
- Candidates may be approved or rejected without destroying the discovery evidence that produced them.
- Approval is rejected unless it is explicitly marked as a human decision.
- A candidate cannot become a canonical Mosaic until after that human approval.
- Materialization gives the Mosaic its deliberate name, description, emoji membership, defining traits, optional Gem, metadata, and provenance back to the source candidate and decision.
- Algorithms and AI still cannot silently create canon.

This is intentionally a decision/model layer. It does not invent Mosaic names, Gems, requirements, or game canon.
