# Emojeo Pass 13 — Evidence Intake & Recovery Bridge

Pass 13 creates the controlled doorway for bringing recovered MASHPEDITION knowledge and other design evidence into Emojeo.

## Added
- Staged evidence batches for Things, Relationships, and Canon Ledger claims.
- Mandatory source provenance before evidence can enter intake.
- Preview with no graph mutation.
- Within-batch duplicate collapse.
- Explicit human approval required before commit.
- Rejected batches remain preserved rather than deleted.
- Every committed record retains source, batch, and intake-item provenance.
- Current canon can enter only through the existing Canon Ledger authority rules.

## Why
Emojeo can now receive recovered project knowledge without turning retrieval output into canon automatically. Recovery evidence can be staged, inspected, accepted or rejected, and traced back to its source.
