# StringBoard Engine v1

This repository is the canonical home of StringBoard Engine.

**Principle:** Applications provide meaning; StringBoard provides capability.

StringBoard Engine v1 is the qualified reusable baseline produced by the Genreactrix extraction through Pass 17. Its reusable mechanisms are application-neutral, while specialized Genreactrix implementations are preserved as encoded know-how and compatibility machinery rather than discarded.

The v1 qualification suite uses a neutral `Field Notes` fixture to demonstrate operation without Genreactrix or emoji vocabulary.
