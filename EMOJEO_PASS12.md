# Emojeo Pass 12 — Canon Ledger & Contradiction Preservation

Pass 12 gives the multi-domain design graph a provenance-aware design ledger.

## Why this exists
MASHPEDITION has current decisions, historical designs, proposals, discoveries, authoritative external facts, retired ideas, and unresolved contradictions. Emojeo must retain those distinctions instead of flattening the newest statement over older evidence.

## Added
- Claims can attach to any Thing in the shared design graph.
- Claim states: proposed, current, historical, retired, rejected, unknown.
- Authority classes remain explicit.
- Current claims require human-canon or authoritative provenance.
- Historical and superseded ideas remain queryable rather than being deleted.
- Conflicting live claims are surfaced as conflicts; Emojeo does not choose a winner.
- Current views refuse to silently collapse contradictory current claims.
- Explicit lifecycle transitions preserve provenance and notes.
- Bulk claim import supports future recovery/import work.

This is infrastructure for importing MASHPEDITION's recovered design knowledge safely while preserving what Billy decided, what used to be true, what is merely proposed, and what remains unresolved.
