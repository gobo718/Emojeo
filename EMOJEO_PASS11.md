# Emojeo Pass 11 — Design Catalog & Connection Vocabulary

Pass 11 makes the Pass 10 graph useful as a MASHPEDITION-wide design model without prematurely encoding individual game content.

## Initial domains
Emoji, Mosaic, Gem, Setting, Unlockable, Achievement, Trophy, Metric, Reward, Item, NPC, Event.

These are extensible Thing types, not closed schemas. Emoji and Mosaic retain their richer existing models.

## Shared connection vocabulary
Requires, Unlocks, Awards, Tracks, Progresses, Participates In, Associated With, Contains, Depends On, Derived From. Existing Mosaic Member remains intact.

## Analysis
The catalog can summarize each domain and surface structural gaps such as disconnected Things, rewards/Gems without a source, unused metrics, and unconnected milestones.

## Canon boundary
The catalog contains no invented MASHPEDITION records. Machine-suggested connections continue to default to advisory under the Pass 10 graph policy; canonical connections require explicit human approval.
