# Emojeo Pass 8 — Feature Intersections + Cluster Discovery

Pass 8 turns Emojeo's established trait assignments into a discovery surface for recurring combinations.

## Added
- Corpus-wide intersections of two or more traits.
- Similarity clustering using Jaccard overlap and connected components.
- Mosaic-candidate projection from discovered clusters.
- Mixed provenance is intentional: Unicode authoritative traits and approved curated traits can participate in the same intersection.
- Candidate results remain advisory. Discovery cannot create a Mosaic, trait, or other canon without an explicit later decision.

## Boundaries
Raw discovery evidence remains untouched. Reclassification decisions remain the source of curated trait presence. Unicode authoritative traits remain authoritative. Pass 8 derives patterns from assignments; it does not rewrite them.
