# Emojeo Pass 15 — Design Health & Attention Queue

Pass 15 turns the existing catalog, canon ledger, and progression analysis into a unified read-only design-health layer.

It surfaces disconnected content, missing sources, unused metrics, unreachable content, dependency cycles, bottlenecks, and canon contradictions in one attention queue. Critical/warning/notice are triage levels only; Emojeo does not automatically repair design or change canon.

The layer also reports per-domain coverage and can focus findings on a single Thing. Advisory progression relationships remain excluded by default through the Pass 14 analysis policy.
