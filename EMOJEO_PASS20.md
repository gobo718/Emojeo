# Emojeo Pass 20 — Practical Multi-Domain Design Workbench

Pass 20 adds a practical, application-neutral workbench surface over the MASHPEDITION design graph.

- Browses every registered design domain, including empty/extensible domains.
- Filters Things by domain, lifecycle status, and free-text identity search.
- Shows connection counts and inspects linked Things in either direction.
- Supports selection/detail rendering without changing graph state.
- Exports the current workbench view for handoff/reporting.
- Keeps navigation and inspection explicitly read-only; canon and graph mutation remain behind the established human-authorized workflows.
- Provides a mountable browser surface rather than coupling the engine to a specific page layout.

No existing Emoji Explorer layout is replaced in this pass and no unrelated refactor is included.
