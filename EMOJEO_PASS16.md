# Emojeo Pass 16 — Change Impact & What-If Preview

Pass 16 adds a read-only change-impact layer above the multi-domain design graph, canon ledger, progression analysis, and design health system.

It can preview retirement of a Thing, a proposed canon-claim change, and connection additions/removals; trace affected content across multiple hops; surface current claims and existing contradictory/historical claims; and carry the live health/progression baseline into a comparison package.

The preview never applies the proposed change. Graph and canon mutation are explicitly forbidden by policy. A human decision remains required before any proposal can become design truth.

Advisory/machine relationships remain excluded from impact traversal by default and can only be included deliberately.
